What makes sustainability science distinctive as a field? This post outlines the core elements of our framework, building on Clark & Harley 2020.

Sustainability science emerged in the early 2000s as a problem-driven field focused on understanding interactions between nature and society. Unlike traditional disciplines, it starts with real-world challenges and draws on whatever methods and perspectives are needed to address them.

The framework centers on six strategic capacities for sustainable development:
- Capacity to measure
- Capacity to value
- Capacity to govern
- Capacity to adapt
- Capacity to transform
- Capacity to link knowledge with action
