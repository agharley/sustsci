Power mediates every relationship in nature-society systems—between actors and resources, between communities and states, between those who set the rules and those who must follow them. Yet sustainability science has been slow to grapple with power directly.

In our framework for research in sustainability science, power sits at the center, mediating the relationships between goals, resources, actors, and institutions. But what do we mean by power? And how should researchers examine it?
