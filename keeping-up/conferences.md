## 4S

**Society for Social Studies of Science (50th Anniversary)**

October 7–10, 2026 | Toronto, Canada (hybrid)

The 50th Anniversary meeting calls for presentations, panels, and adjacent gatherings that engage the theme of "TechnoPower." Science, technology, and innovation are not neutral; as STS scholars, we understand them as socially, culturally, and economically constructed and deeply entangled with a specific form of technoscientific power. A key challenge now is to accelerate our interventions into public, policy, and political debate by using our empirical, analytical, and normative tools to shift technoscientific futures towards a technoscience that fosters collective wellbeing, justice, and sustainability.

- **Deadline:** April 30, 2026 (abstracts)
- **Host:** Society for Social Studies of Science
- **Link:** [4sonline.org/meeting.php](https://www.4sonline.org/meeting.php)

## ESG

**Earth System Governance Conference**

September 7–11, 2026 | Bath, UK

Theme: "Building just and sustainable futures for planetary integrity." The main conference is organized around six streams, including the five analytical lenses of the ESG project: Architecture and Agency; Democracy and Power; Justice and Allocation; Anticipation and Imagination; Adaptiveness and Reflexivity; and a sixth stream on the future of earth system governance. The 2026 Bath Conference will also mark the beginning to develop a new ten-year plan for the Earth System Governance Project.

- **Deadline:** January 15, 2026
- **Host:** University of Bath, Earth System Governance Project
- **Link:** [earthsystemgovernance.org/2026-bath/call-for-papers](https://www.earthsystemgovernance.org/2026-bath/call-for-papers/)

## FLARE

**Forests & Livelihoods: Assessment, Research, and Engagement (12th Annual Meeting)**

October 21–26, 2026 | Bilbao, Spain

The FLARE Network leverages existing expertise and catalyzes new efforts to develop and share leading-edge research and conversations on livelihoods related to forests and tree-based systems. FLARE seeks to support policy development, on-the-ground action, and learning that furthers environment, development, and equity goals in countries across the globe.

- **Deadline:** Early 2026 (call forthcoming)
- **Host:** University of Deusto, FLARE Network Secretariat (University of Notre Dame)
- **Link:** [forestlivelihoods.org](https://www.forestlivelihoods.org/)

## Global EJ

**Global Environmental Justice Conference**

April 13–14, 2026 | Fort Collins, CO (hybrid)

Theme: "Where Do We Grow From Here? Environmental Justice and the Politics of Hope in the Planetary Age." This hybrid gathering relaunches a series of conferences on environmental justice envisioned in 2017 but interrupted by the pandemic. The aim is to deepen connections amongst EJ scholars, advocates, and practitioners across South and North, East and West, across different political and theoretical traditions and substantive interests.

- **Deadline:** Closed
- **Host:** Center for Environmental Justice, Colorado State University
- **Link:** [environmentaljustice.colostate.edu/global-environmental-justice-conference-2026](https://environmentaljustice.colostate.edu/global-environmental-justice-conference-2026/)

## HBCU Climate Change

**Historically Black Colleges and Universities (HBCU) Climate Change Conference (11th Annual)**

March 18–22, 2026 | New Orleans, LA

This conference unites faculty, students, researchers, climate professionals, and environmental justice community members from HBCUs and beyond to bridge academic research with real-world experiences. Topics include climate justice, adaptation, resilience, global climate policy, sustainable transportation, clean energy, green jobs, and community-driven economic development.

- **Deadline:** Closed
- **Host:** Deep South Center for Environmental Justice, Bullard Center at Texas Southern University
- **Link:** [dscej.org/ccc-2026](https://dscej.org/ccc-2026/)

## IST

**International Sustainability Transitions Conference (17th)**

August 31 – September 2, 2026 | Zurich, Switzerland

Theme: "Hope—in challenging times." The conference invites transition scholars from around the world to engage with ideas that foster hope—hope that through our individual and collective work, environmental sustainability and social justice can be cultivated even in times marked by armed conflicts, political polarisation, and mounting planetary crises. The IST conference is the flagship event for the international transitions research community.

- **Deadline:** February 2026 (TBA)
- **Host:** ETH Zurich, Eawag Cirus Group, Sustainability Transitions Research Network (STRN)
- **Link:** [transitionsnetwork.org/ist-2026](https://www.transitionsnetwork.org/ist-2026/)
