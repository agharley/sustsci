Sustainability Science

Resources for research and teaching on sustainable development

Course

Sustainable Development: A Course Linking Science with Practice. Open-access course materials exploring how peoples of an increasingly stressed planet can achieve better lives for themselves, their neighbors, and their posterity.

The Sustainability Theorist

Essays on the conceptual foundations of sustainability science. Clarifying frameworks, untangling debates, and developing the theoretical tools the field needs.

Keeping Up with the Field

Strategies and resources for tracking new developments in sustainability science—journals, search strategies, key organizations, and active research programs.

Capacity Building for Sustainable Development

The C4SD research project and seminar series. Exploring six strategic capacities for sustainable development through empirical research and practitioner dialogue.

This site is edited by Alicia Harley and William C. Clark as part of the Sustainability Science Program at Harvard Kennedy School.

The SSP works to advance scientific understanding of human-environment systems, strengthen connections between research and policy communities, and build capacity in the next generation of sustainability scientists. For more on the program's history, initiatives, and people, visit the SSP page at Harvard Kennedy School.
